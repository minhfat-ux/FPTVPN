## Full description (max 4000 chars)
VPNFlow is a fast, privacy-first VPN built to stay connected on networks where ordinary VPNs give up.

WHY VPNFLOW
• Hysteria2 engine — a modern transport built for speed and stability
• Smart fallback — if UDP is blocked (hotel Wi-Fi, campus, office networks, mobile carriers), VPNFlow switches automatically to a TCP tunnel and keeps working
• Choose your exit server right in the app
• Reconnects automatically when the network drops
• No activity logs — we do not record the sites you visit

HOW IT WORKS
1. Sign in with your email
2. Pick a server
3. Tap Connect — that's it

PRIVACY
We keep the minimum needed to run the service (your account email and the device identity that gets your tunnel address). We do not log browsing activity, and we never sell your data.
